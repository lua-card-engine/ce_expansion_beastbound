# Beastbound TCG — Card List

All rules referenced here (types, status effects, damage math, rarity) are defined in [game-rules.md](game-rules.md).

Attack costs are a number of Energy **of the Beast's own type** (this set has no colorless energy). Damage shown is the attack's base/printed damage, before Weakness/Resistance is applied.
